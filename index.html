Dispute Resolution | Mediation & Arbitration

LAWGUARDIAN believes in effective courtroom advocacy and in alternative dispute resolution. The right strategy depends on the nature of the dispute and the client’s interests, and not every dispute is suited to settlement outside court.

Mediation

Facilitating amicable settlements through structured dialogue, and helping parties explore mutually acceptable solutions.

Arbitration

Representation and assistance in arbitration proceedings, resolving disputes outside conventional court litigation.

Alternative Dispute Resolution

Efficient, practical and legally sound approaches to resolving disputes, wherever appropriate.

Whether a matter calls for the courtroom, the mediation table or an arbitral tribunal, the firm can assist clients at each stage.
